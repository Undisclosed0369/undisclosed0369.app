## Tap reactions (SwiftMediaInfo icon)

The icon wiggles a little more on each tap. A tiny speech bubble shows the line.

| Tap | Line |
|---|---|
| 1 | *(nothing — just a wiggle)* |
| 2 | *(nothing — slightly bigger wiggle)* |
