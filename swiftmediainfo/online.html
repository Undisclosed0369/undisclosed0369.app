Where your file goes

                    

                        Nowhere. MediaInfo is compiled to WebAssembly and runs inside
                        this page, on your own machine. Your file is read by the
                        browser and handed straight to it.
                    

                


                

                    
How to check

                    

                        Open your browser's network tab and drop a file in. You will
                        see the analyser load once, and nothing after it. There is no
                        server here to upload to — this site is static files.
                    

                

            


            

                The analyser is about 2.5 MB and is fetched only when you pick a
                file, from this domain rather than someone else's. Large files are
                read in pieces rather than loaded whole, so a 40 GB disc image
                is no more trouble than a ringtone.
            


            
            

                And it is MediaInfo itself, not an imitation of it. Every number you
                see was read by
                MediaInfo,
                an open-source project by
                MediaArea,
                compiled to WebAssembly by the
                mediainfo.js
                project. They run
                a browser
                version of their own as well, with more output formats than the
                four here — and if any of this proves useful, they are the people who
                deserve
                supporting.
